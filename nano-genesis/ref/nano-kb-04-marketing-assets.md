Source: NANO_GENESIS_Commercial.txt section 4 + core-07-NANO_GENESIS.md Section 4.

## Podcast Cover Art
- Resolution 3000x3000px, 1:1, JPG/PNG sRGB, **under 512KB** (Apple Podcasts hard requirement).
- Safe zone: keep critical text/imagery in center 80% (outer 10% may crop on some platforms) — i.e. within roughly 2400x2400 of a 3000x3000 canvas.
- Design: large bold readable-at-thumbnail text (test at 300x300px), high contrast for dark/light mode, clean simple design, consistent with brand.
- Layout: title upper two-thirds centered, tagline below, visual element (mic icon/waveform) bottom third or subtle background, don't let icon compete with text.

## YouTube / Video Thumbnails
- Resolution 1280x720px min (16:9), format JPG/PNG/GIF/BMP, under 2MB.
- Faces with expressive emotion drive clicks. Text: max 3-5 words, bold thick sans-serif (Impact/Montserrat Bold/Bebas Neue), strong outline/shadow for legibility over any background, bright contrasting colors.
- No clickbait — thumbnail must accurately represent content.
- Layout pattern: person/face with excited expression on one side (left third typically), key-promise text overlay on the other, accent graphics (arrows, icons) pointing at the promise. Test legibility at 320x180px (mobile thumbnail size).

## Social Media Ad Formats by Platform
| Platform | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Feed | 1080x1080 (1:1) or 1080x1350 (4:5) | Imagery-focused, caption does the copy work |
| Instagram Story | 1080x1920 (9:16) | Safe zone center 1080x1420 (avoid top/bottom cutoff) |
| Facebook Feed | 1200x628 (1.91:1) or 1080x1080 | Headline ≤40 chars, description ≤30 chars |
| LinkedIn Sponsored | 1200x627 (1.91:1) or 1080x1080 | Professional tone, B2B imagery, clear value prop |

Ad JSON structure: campaign goal + target audience + CTA, format/safe-zone, hero-image lifestyle description with sharp product focal point, text overlay (headline/product-name/CTA with placement + color), color palette, photography style (natural lighting, shallow DOF f/2.8-f/4, rule of thirds, authenticity), branding (small logo, brand colors). Keep text-in-image under ~20% of image area for best ad performance.

## Quote Graphics & Social Content
1080x1080 square. Structure: background (minimal gradient/solid/brand colors), quote text (serif or elegant sans-serif, large 60-80pt equivalent, high contrast, generous line spacing), attribution (smaller, lighter weight, em-dash before name), optional accent (decorative quote marks, geometric shapes, subtle logo). Safe zone: keep text within center ~85% of canvas.

Color-scheme presets:
- **Professional**: navy bg (#1A2B4C), white/cream text, gold/teal accent
- **Modern**: purple-to-blue gradient bg, white text, bright cyan accent
- **Minimal**: off-white bg (#F8F8F8), dark gray/black text, brand-color accent
