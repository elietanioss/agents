# PM KB 01 — Strategic Portfolio Management

## Pattern 1.1: Portfolio Prioritization Matrix

**Tier 1: Strategic Priority Projects (40-50% of resources)** — direct alignment with top 3 business objectives, 30%+ expected ROI, competitive-advantage impact, executive sponsor commitment. Gets best available talent, premium budget + contingency, fast-track approval.

**Tier 2: Growth & Optimization Projects (30-40% of resources)** — 15-30% expected ROI, process improvement or gradual growth, standard business justification. Experienced team member, standard budget process, normal approval workflow.

**Tier 3: Innovation & Learning Projects (10-20% of resources)** — experimental/proof-of-concept, technology evaluation, market testing. Junior/mid talent with senior mentorship, stage-gate funding, rapid iteration / fail-fast.

**Portfolio balance guidelines:**
- Risk distribution: 60% low-risk / 30% medium-risk / 10% high-risk
- Timeline distribution: 40% short-term (0-3mo) / 40% medium-term (3-9mo) / 20% long-term (9mo+)
- Capacity planning: 70% committed / 20% planned pipeline / 10% reserve for unexpected opportunities

**When to use:** start of fiscal quarters/years, evaluating new proposals against the existing portfolio, resource-constrained prioritization decisions.

## Pattern 1.2: Strategic Roadmap Development

Quarterly template: Executive Summary (vision, portfolio value/ROI, market opportunity) → Q1-Q4 breakdown each with a strategic theme, major milestones (deliverable, success criteria, dependencies, risk level), resource allocation (FTEs by discipline, budget) → cross-quarter dependency chain diagram → strategic risk assessment (market/execution/resource risks with impact×probability) → success metrics/KPIs (financial, delivery, strategic) → quarterly review gates (health check, budget vs actual, go/no-go decisions).

**Implementation process (7 weeks):**
1. Strategic input gathering (wks 1-2): executive interviews, market analysis, customer feedback, technical capability assessment
2. Roadmap drafting (wks 3-4): project identification, dependency mapping, capacity planning, budget modeling
3. Stakeholder alignment (wks 5-6): executive presentation, department alignment, cross-functional negotiation, approval
4. Communication & rollout (wk 7): all-hands presentation, team planning sessions, kickoffs

## Pattern 1.3: ROI Calculation & Business Case Framework

**Structure:** Executive summary (recommendation, investment, expected return, ROI%, payback period) → investment breakdown by category (dev/tech/ops costs, one-time vs recurring, 3-year total) → expected returns (revenue growth, cost savings, quantified per category) → 3-year financial projection table → strategic/intangible benefits (competitive advantage, CX, operational excellence, risk mitigation) → ROI analysis:

```
ROI = (Net Benefit / Investment) × 100
Payback period = month cumulative cash flow turns positive
```

**Sensitivity analysis** — weight 3 scenarios (Conservative 30% / Baseline 50% / Optimistic 20%) and compute risk-adjusted ROI as the probability-weighted average. **Comparison table** vs alternatives (investment, 3-yr ROI, payback, strategic value) including "Do Nothing" as a baseline option.

**Approval criteria (all must hold):** ROI > 25%, payback < 18 months, aligns with top-3 business objectives, risk-adjusted ROI > 20%, resources confirmed available.

**When to use:** proposing new strategic initiatives, budget justification, comparing solution options, annual planning, post-project retrospective validation of original predictions.
