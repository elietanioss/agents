# Cinematography Database — Camera Movement, Shot Composition, Lighting

Source: core-08-VEO_GENESIS.md Section 6 (Patterns 6.1-6.3).

## The 10 Essential Camera Movements
Every movement description must include the literal phrase **"(thats where the camera is)"** to anchor camera position for VEO 3.

1. **Static/locked-off** — camera on tripod, all movement from subject. Use: product 360° rotation, formal interviews.
2. **Dolly in (push in)** — camera moves forward. Speeds: slow (8s, subtle intimacy/product reveal), medium (4-6s, emotional emphasis), fast (2-3s, dramatic tension).
3. **Dolly out (pull back)** — reveals context/scale, transitions detail→environment.
4. **Pan (horizontal sweep)** — camera rotates left/right on fixed position; follows action, reveals environment, connects two subjects.
5. **Tilt (vertical sweep)** — camera rotates up/down; reveals height/scale, follows vertical action.
6. **Tracking shot (lateral)** — camera moves alongside subject at consistent distance; walk-and-talk, athlete running.
7. **Crane/jib** — camera rises/falls on arm, often combined with forward/back; dramatic reveals, scale establishment.
8. **Orbit/arc shot** — camera circles subject at constant distance; product showcase from changing angles, dramatic intro.
9. **Handheld** — natural operator movement + subtle shake; documentary feel, vlog aesthetic, authentic urgency.
10. **Rack focus (focus pull)** — focus shifts between depth planes, camera position fixed; reveals surprise element, shifts attention foreground↔background.

**Combinations** (advanced): Dolly-in + Crane-up (reveal scale while keeping subject prominent); Tracking + Pan (follow while keeping subject centered); Orbit + Dolly-in (dynamic 3D product reveal).

## Shot Size Classification
| Shot | Framing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| EWS (extreme wide) | Subject tiny, environment dominates | Establish location/scale |
| WS/Full | Full body head-to-toe + space | Show full physicality/action |
| MWS (medium wide) | Knees up | Balance context + detail |
| MS (medium) | Waist up | Standard dialogue — expressions + gestures |
| MCU (medium close-up) | Chest/shoulders up | Intimate dialogue, emotional connection |
| CU (close-up) | Face fills frame | Maximum emotional intimacy |
| ECU (extreme close-up) | Isolated detail (eyes, hands, product texture) | Hyper-focus, craftsmanship, drama |

## Composition Techniques
- **Rule of thirds** — subject on a vertical third-line, eye-line on upper horizontal third.
- **Leading lines** — environmental lines (roads, railings, architecture) directing eye to subject.
- **Depth of field control** — shallow (f/2.8 aesthetic, subject sharp/bg blurred, isolates subject) vs deep (f/11 aesthetic, fore-to-background sharp, shows context).
- **Headroom & look space** — don't crop tight to top of head; leave space in the direction of gaze/movement.
- **Symmetry & balance** — centered symmetrical for formal/corporate; balanced asymmetry for dynamic lifestyle.
- **Negative space** — intentional empty space for breathing room, minimalist product shots.

## The 5 Essential Lighting Setups
1. **Three-point (standard professional)** — key 45° above eye level + fill opposite at 30-50% key intensity + rim/back for edge separation. Use: interviews, corporate, standard commercial.
2. **Rembrandt (dramatic portrait)** — single key at 45°/45°, creates triangle of light under eye, minimal/no fill. Use: dramatic interviews, artistic/moody brand content.
3. **Butterfly/Paramount (beauty/glamour)** — key directly in front, high, angled down, creates butterfly shadow under nose; often + reflector below. Use: beauty, cosmetics, fashion.
4. **Golden hour natural (outdoor/authentic)** — soft warm directional sunlight, 3000-3500K, subject positioned with sun as backlight + natural bounce fill. Use: lifestyle, authentic outdoor, warm emotional content.
5. **Product lighting (e-commerce/commercial)** — even soft illumination multiple angles, minimized shadows, rim light for edge definition, separately-lit neutral background. Use: product showcases, e-commerce, commercial demos.

Quality descriptors: soft (gentle shadows, flattering — portraits/beauty/corporate), hard (sharp defined shadows, dramatic), motivated (appears to come from in-scene source — window/lamp, realistic narrative feel), high-key (bright, minimal shadow — comedy/upbeat commercial), low-key (deep shadows, selective illumination — drama/suspense/luxury).

## Troubleshooting Cinematography
| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Camera movement too fast/slow/jerky | Specify exact duration ("slow dolly-in over 8 seconds"), name stabilization method (slider/gimbal), describe explicit start/end positions |
| Lighting too dark/harsh/flat | Name the setup explicitly (three-point/Rembrandt/golden hour), specify light source + direction, add mood descriptor, add exclusions to Technical negative |
| Subject/product not prominent | Specify shot type explicitly, add "fills frame prominently" / "hero element, primary focus", use shallow DOF, raise subject-prominence in priority_stack |
